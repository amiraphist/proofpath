# Ledger Nano Gen5 — Product-reference notes

## Confirmed product naming

The official Ledger shop names the device **Ledger Nano™ Gen5**. The spelling requested for the game, “Nano Gen5,” is therefore supported by Ledger’s own product page.

## Safe educational use in ProofPath

The game may depict a simplified **Ledger Nano™ Gen5 secure signer** card in its simulated payment flows. The card should teach one bounded concept: an AI agent can prepare a payment, but a person must verify the recipient and amount on a trusted device before simulated signing occurs. The game must show that it is a simulation and must not claim a connection to Ledger software, a Ledger partnership, or real transaction execution.

## Product details appropriate for a neutral teaching card

Ledger’s official product page describes an EAL6+ Secure Element and a 2.8-inch touchscreen intended to verify details at a glance. In the game, these become plain-language labels: **check the recipient**, **check the amount**, and **press to sign**.
