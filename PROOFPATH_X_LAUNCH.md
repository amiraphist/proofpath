## post 1

i spent over roughly 48 hours vibe coding my first small educational game, with no programming background.

meet proofpath, a short game about building safer payment paths for ai agents.

play it: https://amiraphist.github.io/proofpath/

## post 2

i built it from zero to public launch inside @manusai.

i occasionally asked claude and grok when i got stuck, but most of the actual building happened in manus.

it turned out to be way more fun than i expected.

## post 3

proofpath has 16 short build + fix missions.

you create or repair safe payment routes using policy checks, approvals, action seals, and a simulated hardware signer.

simple on purpose. learn by moving the pieces.

## post 4

i used a simulated signer inspired by @ledger, based on the ledger nano gen5 because i genuinely love that model.

one idea matters: before money moves, a human should be able to verify what will happen. no real money moves. just practice.
