## post 3

proofpath has 16 short build + fix missions. you create or repair safe payment routes with things like policy checks, approvals, action seals, and a simulated hardware signer.

simple on purpose. learn by moving the pieces.

## post 4

i used a simulated signer inspired by @ledger because one idea matters: before money moves, a human should be able to verify what will happen.

it’s just an educational game. no real money moves. just practice.
