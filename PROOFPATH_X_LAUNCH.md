## post 3

proofpath has 16 short build + fix missions.

you create or repair safe payment routes using policy checks, approvals, action seals, and a simulated hardware signer.

simple on purpose. learn by moving the pieces.

## post 4

i used a simulated signer inspired by @ledger, based on the ledger nano gen5 because i genuinely love that model.

one idea matters: before money moves, a human should be able to verify what will happen. no real money moves. just practice.
